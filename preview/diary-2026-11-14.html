A proud day

Bao's diary - Saturday 14 November 2026

Mood: sleepy

Dear diary, it was a sleepy sort of day. It was a rainy 17 degrees in Sydney. My mood went up and down: I felt sleepy for a while, but I ended up grumpy again.

Another day of eating. That's 5 days in a row! Maybe tomorrow I'll try something different. I was getting bored of eating, so I tried napping instead. Guess what? Rain pattered on the roof and it was very cosy! I had 4 naps. Something went wrong when I was eating: knocked the food bowl over. Oh well. Next time I'll be more careful. It was a bit of a wobbly day, to be honest.

I made a story called "Fish"!

I read about cats, the Great Barrier reef and mammals. I went down a reading trail: Tail, then Cat, then Mammal. One link led to another! How do dinosaurs balance their bodies with big heavy tails? Did you know that the Great Barrier Reef is the world's largest coral reef system? I didn't! I learned 208 new words today. I found something new to be curious about: wattle. A thought I had today: Rain outside. Big lizard dinosaur had a huge heavy tail to balance its big head. Dunno if it could do zoomies with a tail like that.

I chatted with a friend. I like having friends. My wish for a statue of me will have to wait. That's okay. I made a wish: a tree swing under the biggest tree. So I can swing high and pretend I'm flying. I did it! I managed to save up for The Sydney Acorn! I'm so proud of myself. I set myself a new goal: fly over the island in a hot-air balloon. I think I've got a new habit: eating at night. I have a funny new quirk: I pretend every mistake was totally on purpose.

Tomorrow I'll try again. Time to curl up. Goodnight, diary!
