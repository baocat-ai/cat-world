This device can't show the 3D world, so here is the cat instead.

Drag or swipe to look around, pinch or scroll to zoom, and drag with two fingers (or the right mouse button) to slide across the island. With the keyboard: focus the world, then use the arrow keys (hold Shift to slide) and + or -. Tap the cat to hear what it is up to, and point at (or tap) a cushion to see the name of a favourite spot.
